CycleLedger Privacy Policy

1. Scope and developer

This policy applies to CycleLedger for Android (com.mrongm.cycleledger), published by MRongM. Contact: jiangisright@gmail.com. CycleLedger is a local ledger with no app account, cloud synchronization, or developer-operated backend. The app is free to download; CSV export is an optional one-time lifetime purchase through Google Play.

2. Ledger and preferences on your device

Amounts, income and expense records, dates, categories and notes are stored in a private local Room database. Theme, billing-cycle start day and language preferences are stored using Android DataStore. A verified purchase status is stored separately for offline export access. The app does not upload your ledger or preferences to MRongM, and includes no advertising or app analytics service.

3. Android backup and device transfer

Android backup is enabled. Your database and ordinary DataStore preferences may be included in cloud backup or device transfer according to your Android settings and backup provider. The verified purchase preferences store is excluded from both configured backup policies. Cached CSV export files are excluded from backup. Android and the device or backup provider control these services; their own privacy policies apply. Restoring a backup does not itself grant the paid export entitlement.

4. CSV export and sharing

When you choose CSV export, the app creates a temporary CSV file in private cache storage containing the selected ledger information. The Android Sharesheet grants read access to the receiving app you select. That app or service may save or upload the file under its own policy. Opening the chooser does not confirm delivery or saving. Temporary files can remain in cache until cleaned by the app or Android; clearing the app cache removes cached exports. You control any copies saved outside CycleLedger.

5. Google Play purchases and diagnostics

Google Play processes checkout, payment, purchase records and related service or diagnostic information under Google’s policies. CycleLedger uses Play Billing to query the export product, launch checkout, acknowledge purchases and restore ownership. The app receives product prices, purchase tokens and purchase or acknowledgement status to verify export access; it does not receive your payment card details. The app does not send ledger contents to Google Play Billing. Google Play and Android may process purchase, device, usage or diagnostic information according to their services and your settings. The app declares no INTERNET permission; Google Play services and an external browser can communicate separately. See Google’s privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. External privacy page and support

Opening this policy launches your external browser. The website host and browser may process ordinary web request information such as IP address and browser metadata. If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to provide are processed to respond. Avoid sending sensitive ledger information unless needed for your request.

7. Your choices, retention and deletion

You can edit or delete ledger records and manage categories in the app. Clear Android app storage to remove local ledger, settings and cached purchase status; uninstalling normally removes app-private local data. Backups and exported copies may remain with their respective providers or recipients and must be managed separately. Google Play retains purchase records under its own policies; clearing or uninstalling the app does not cancel or erase a purchase. Restore purchases can recover eligible ownership through Google Play.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or relevant services change. The current version and update date will appear here. Developer: MRongM. Privacy and support email: jiangisright@gmail.com.
