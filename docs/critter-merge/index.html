Critter Merge Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 Developer: MRongM Contact: 1399316847@qq.com Package name: com.mrongm.mergelong

Critter Merge is a game that stores gameplay locally and may offer optional rewarded ads and an optional in-app purchase. This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your high score and current game session, including the score, next critter, and the positions, velocities and levels of critters on the board. It also stores a local cache of your unlimited-hammer purchase status. These records let you resume play and use an existing entitlement while offline. The game does not send the saved score or session to the developer's servers.

Advertising and privacy choices

If you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement, Google AdMob provides the advertisement. Google Mobile Ads, Google Play services and the Google User Messaging Platform may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic information to provide and measure ads, prevent fraud, and operate their services. Depending on your location and applicable choices, Google may ask for consent before ads are requested. You can open Privacy options from the game's pause screen where applicable. Advertising and consent services require an internet connection.

Google describes its practices and available controls in its Privacy Policy and advertising information.

Purchases

Google Play processes optional in-app purchases and provides the app with purchase status. The developer does not receive your payment card number. An unlimited-hammer purchase is associated with the Google Play account used to buy it and can be restored when the app reconnects to that account. Google Play may process account, transaction and device information under its own policies. The app itself does not require an account.

Retention and deletion

Your locally saved game, high score and cached entitlement remain in app storage until you clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall it. A purchase remains associated with your Google Play account and may be restored after reinstalling. To manage Google account information and purchase history, use Google Play and Google account settings. We do not operate a server-side game account or retain your game session on our servers.

Children

The game is not specifically directed at children. Google Play's target audience and the advertising settings for the app are configured in the store listing. If you have questions about a child's use of the app, please contact us.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above indicates when this version took effect. For questions, contact 1399316847@qq.com.

萌兽合合乐隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日 开发者：MRongM 联系邮箱：1399316847@qq.com 包名：com.mrongm.mergelong

《萌兽合合乐》是一款在设备本地保存游戏进度的游戏，可能提供可选激励广告和应用内购买。

本地数据

应用在设备上保存最高分和当前游戏状态，包括分数、待投放角色及场内角色的位置、速度和等级； 还会缓存无限锤子购买状态，以便恢复游戏并在离线时使用已有权益。游戏不会将分数或续局状态 发送到开发者服务器。

广告与隐私选择

用户选择观看激励广告时，广告由 Google AdMob 提供。Google 移动广告 SDK、Google Play 服务 和 Google User Messaging Platform 可能处理设备标识符、IP 地址、广告互动和诊断信息，用于 提供和衡量广告、防范欺诈及运行服务。根据所在地区和适用选择，Google 可能在请求广告前 征求同意。适用时可从游戏暂停界面进入“隐私选项”。广告和同意服务需要网络连接。

详情请参阅 Google 隐私权政策和 Google 广告说明。

应用内购买

可选购买由 Google Play 处理，Google Play 向应用提供购买状态；开发者不会收到用户的银行卡号。 无限锤子购买与购买时使用的 Google Play 账号关联，应用重新连接该账号后可恢复购买。Google 可能依据其政策处理账号、交易及设备信息。应用本身无需注册账号。

保留与删除

本地游戏数据、最高分和权益缓存保留在应用存储中，直到用户在 Android 设置中清除应用数据或 卸载应用。购买记录仍与 Google Play 账号关联，重新安装后可能恢复。Google 账号信息和购买 记录可通过 Google Play 与 Google 账号设置管理。开发者不运营服务端游戏账号，也不在服务器上 保存用户的游戏进度。

儿童、政策变更与联系

本游戏不专门面向儿童；目标受众和广告设置在 Google Play 商品详情中配置。应用或数据处理方式 变化时，开发者可能更新本政策，并调整顶部生效日期。如有疑问，请联系 1399316847@qq.com。
