Privacy policy

Love Rescue stores game progress on your device and uses Google services for advertising, privacy choices and optional purchases.

Last updated: 2026-11-08 · Developer: MRongM

1. Scope

This policy describes how the Android game Love Rescue (package com.mrongm.loverescue), provided by MRongM, handles information. The game does not require a game account. Game progress and settings are stored on the device.

2. Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, rewards, settings and language preferences locally, together with a game-generated local identifier. Android or a device backup service may retain copies when backup is enabled. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data; backups are managed separately through your device or backup provider.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK for optional rewarded advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage privacy choices where required. Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares IP address, product and advertising interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. IP address may be used to estimate general location. Depending on your choices and the configuration, Google may share information with advertising partners.

Google explains its practices and retention in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Optional purchases

The optional one-time purchase to remove ads uses Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the purchase under its own terms and privacy policy. To check and restore the entitlement, the game receives purchase status and signed purchase information, including a purchase token, and stores verified purchase information locally. Google Play handles payment details; MRongM does not receive your payment-card details through the game.

5. Retention, choices and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until you clear app storage or uninstall, subject to separate device or cloud backups. Verified purchase information is kept locally while needed to provide the entitlement and may be refreshed or removed after a successful Google Play purchase query. Google retains information it receives according to its own policies. When required, an advertising privacy-options control is available in the game's settings. You can also manage the advertising ID through Android settings and Google advertising choices through your Google account.

For questions or requests about information held by MRongM, email the contact below. A parent or guardian with concerns about a child's information may also contact us. Google provides its own controls for information it processes.

6. Security

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts the user data it collects in transit. The game uses Android app storage for local game data. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or applicable requirements change. For privacy questions, contact MRongM at jiangisright@gmail.com.
