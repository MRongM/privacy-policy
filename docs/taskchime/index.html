TaskChime Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-28 · 简体中文

Data collection and sharing

Reminder titles, spoken notes, schedules, and settings stay in private app storage on the device and are never sent to a server. TaskChime has no publisher account, advertising, analytics, microphone, or location feature. MRongM does not operate a TaskChime server and does not receive or sell reminder content or payment card details.

Optional purchase and restoration

The app is free to download with an allowance of three unfinished reminders. An optional one-time purchase permanently unlocks the full version. Google Play processes payments, purchase verification, acknowledgement, and restoration under Google's terms and privacy policy. Buying or restoring requires connectivity to Google Play. TaskChime receives signed purchase details and purchase identifiers from Google Play and stores verified purchase evidence locally to retain full access offline. The app does not ask for payment card details or send reminder text to Google Play.

After reinstalling or changing devices, restore the purchase using the same Google Play account. Google may retain transaction records under its own policies, independently of uninstalling TaskChime. See Google's privacy policy.

Device capabilities

The app uses Android exact alarms, notifications, temporary foreground media playback, alarm-output routing, and boot restoration so reminders can be delivered on time. These capabilities run on the device and are not used to collect or transmit personal data.

Spoken reminder text is processed by the installed offline Android text-to-speech engine and never leaves the device. The speech engine provider's own policy applies to that engine.

Retention and deletion

You can delete individual reminders in the app. Uninstalling TaskChime removes app-private data according to Android. There is no TaskChime account or server-side copy requiring a separate deletion request.
