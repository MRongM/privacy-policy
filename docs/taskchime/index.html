TaskChime Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-28 · 简体中文

Data collection and sharing

TaskChime is an offline reminder utility. It does not collect, sell, or share personal data. It has no account, advertising, analytics, network, microphone, or location feature. Reminder titles, spoken notes, schedules, and settings stay in private app storage on the device and are never sent to a server.

Device capabilities

The app uses Android exact alarms, notifications, temporary foreground media playback, alarm-output routing, and boot restoration so reminders can be delivered on time. These capabilities run on the device and are not used to collect or transmit personal data.

Spoken reminder text is processed by the installed offline Android text-to-speech engine and never leaves the device. The speech engine provider's own policy applies to that engine.

Retention and deletion

You can delete individual reminders in the app. Uninstalling TaskChime removes app-private data according to Android. There is no TaskChime account or server-side copy requiring a separate deletion request.

Contact

TaskChime is published by MRongM. For privacy questions, use the contact method listed on the Google Play store listing.
