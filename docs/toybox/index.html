Privacy policy

Toybox Battle stores game progress on your device and uses Google services to provide advertising and privacy choices.

Last updated: 2026-11-02 · Developer: MRongM

1. Scope

This policy describes how the Android game Toybox Battle, published by MRongM, handles information. The game does not require a game account and MRongM does not operate a game backend for game progress.

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress, preferences and settings are stored locally on your device. Android or your device's backup service may also retain a copy if backup is enabled. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game removes its local app data; backups are managed separately through your device or backup provider.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and show ads, including optional rewarded ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage privacy choices where required. Google says the Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares the device's IP address, device or account identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. The IP address may be used to estimate general location. This information is used for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Google may share information with advertising partners as described in its own policies.

Google explains its data practices in the Google Privacy Policy and how its services use data from partner applications in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Google manages the retention and deletion of information it receives under those policies.

4. Your choices and deletion

When a privacy options control is required, it is available in the game's settings. You can use your Android settings to reset or delete the advertising ID where supported, and manage other Google advertising choices through your Google account. You can delete locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app; review your device backup settings separately. For questions or requests about the game, contact us using the address below. Google provides its own controls for data it processes under its privacy policy.

5. Security

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts the user data it collects in transit. We use Android's app storage for local game data. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

6. Intended audience

Toybox Battle is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or applicable requirements change. The date above identifies the latest update. For privacy questions, contact MRongM at jiangisright@gmail.com.
